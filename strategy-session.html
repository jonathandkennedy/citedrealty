Why brokers book this

Your agents' lead problem is a visibility problem.

Every broker we talk to describes some version of the same thing. Recruiting is easier when agents believe the brokerage generates business. Retention is easier when it actually does. But the lead flow is rented — portal leads that get sold to three agents at once, ad spend that stops working the day it stops, and a website nobody can find.

Meanwhile the way people pick an agent moved again. Buyers and sellers now ask an assistant — ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, or the AI answer sitting on top of Google — "who's the best agent in [our city]?" Those systems name specific people and specific brokerages. Somebody in your market is already the answer to that question.

The question is whether it's your team.

That's what this hour is for. We come with the research already done, tell you plainly what we found, and leave you with a plan you could execute without us. Not because we're generous — because a broker who can see the gap is a broker who does something about it, and we'd rather be the agency you call once you can see it.

Before the call

We ask the AI about your market

We run the questions your next client is actually asking — "best real estate agent in [your city]", "who should I list with in [neighborhood]", "top brokerage in [market]" — through ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, and Google's AI. Minutes 0–15

The findings, unedited

We show you what we found. If a competing brokerage owns every answer in your market, we say so and show you why. If your own site is invisible to the systems doing the recommending, we show you the specific reasons — and they're usually fixable.

Minutes 15–35

Where your traffic and leads are leaking

Your website, your Google Business Profile, your neighborhood coverage, your reviews, and your agents' individual presence — reviewed as one system. Most brokerages are losing business at a handful of specific, boring, fixable points. We name them.

The plan, in priority order

What to fix first, second, and third, scaled to what your team can realistically execute. We start with what you can do in-house for free, because those are usually the highest-return moves and it tells you fast whether we know what we're talking about.

Minutes 55–60

Your questions — and an honest answer about fit

Then we tell you plainly whether this is something you should run in-house, hire elsewhere for, or bring us in on. Our pricing is public, so there's nothing to reveal at the end. If we're not the right fit, that's a fine outcome for an hour.

Fit

We'd rather use the hour well than fill the calendar.

An hour is real time for both of us. Here's who gets something out of it and who doesn't.

Book it if…

  • You're a broker, brokerage owner, or team lead responsible for where your agents' business comes from.
  • You already have a website and a Google Business Profile and suspect you're getting less from them than you should.
  • Your lead flow is mostly rented — portals, paid leads, or ad spend that stops the day the card stops.
  • You want to recruit and retain on the strength of the business the brokerage generates.
  • You're willing to hear that some of the problem is fixable in-house, and to actually do it.

Skip it if…

  • You want leads next week. This is compounding work — foundations move in weeks, authority takes months. We won't pretend otherwise.
  • You're looking for someone to run ads only. That's a real service; it isn't this one.
  • You want a guarantee of AI placement. Nobody can honestly promise that, and anyone who does is guessing.
  • You're a solo agent just starting out — take the free AI visibility check instead; it's faster and better suited to you.
  • You're a buyer or seller looking for an agent. We market for agents; we don't sell homes.
